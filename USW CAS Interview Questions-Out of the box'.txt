USW CAS Interview Questions
1. What are the three things that appeal to you most about studying in the UK?
2. What are the UKVI rules regarding working while studying in the UK?
3. What is your monthly budget breakdown, including accommodation, food, groceries, and other expenses?
4. Have you faced any challenges or difficulties in finding accommodation?
5. Do you have any plans to work part-time in the UK?
6. How have you developed your study techniques and time-management skills?
7. How is this course relevant to your previous studies and career goals?
8. If you had the opportunity to visit a new country, which country would you choose and why?
9. Describe a memorable journey or trip you took when you were younger.
10. Will you bring any dependants or other family members with you?
11. How will this course help you achieve financial success and build a successful career after graduation?
12. Have you ever had a visa refusal?
13. Which character would you like to play in a movie with?
14. What role did your family, friends, and educational advisor play in your decision-making?
15. If you had the chance to introduce a new law in the UK, what would it be?
16. How will you manage future changes in the job market compared with other students who have graduated from this university?
17. What advice would you give to other students before choosing a university?
18. How will you manage currency fluctuations to cover your education and living expenses?
19. How will you manage your accommodation? Have you already booked accommodation, or will you stay in a homestay?
20. If you could win an Olympic medal for any sport, real or fake, what would it be and why?
21. Are you aware of the working hour limits on a student visa, and how will this affect your financial plans?
22. What's your plan for finding a job and making contacts in your field after you finish your studies?
23. How will you stay connected with your friends and colleagues after completing graduation?
24. How is your academic background related to your intended study?
25. Who is sponsoring your studies, and how will they financially support you during your studies in the UK?
26. How will you stay connected with the residents in your accommodation and the local community in the UK?
27. Do you know what the rules are for students from other countries working in the UK?
28. Have you researched any clubs, societies or voluntary opportunities at the University of South Wales?
29. How important is time management according to you?
30. What is success according to you? Describe.
31. How will you use the contacts that you would make at USW?
32. What specific module will you find difficult?
33. What social and cultural facilities do you think you will get in the UK?
34. How have you demonstrated leadership, teamwork, or problem-solving skills in your academic or employment history?
35. Do you like to live with roommates, housemates, or alone? Why?
36. Which city in the UK do you like, and why would you want to visit it during your free time alongside your studies? Also, what specific aspects of the city attract you?
37. Can you tell us about a time when you faced a significant academic challenge and how you overcame it?
38. If you had to start a business, what would it be?
39. If you got a chance to become an animal, which animal would it be and why?
40. How do you plan to make friends and connect with teachers and peers?
41. How will you use advice from teachers and classmates to do better in your classes?
42. Do you know the university location?
43. Who is the head of your department or course leader?
44. How much are your living expenses expected to be?
45. How did the university location or campus environment affect your study?
46. What advice will you give to your juniors?
47. What skills do you expect to gain from this course?
48. What aspects of the university's campus and facilities do you find most appealing?
49. As you are applying under a Tier 4 visa, do you know the rules and regulations of this?
50. What extracurricular activities do you plan to join at USW?
51. What is your favourite Bangladeshi cuisine, and what UK cuisine would you like to try while studying here?
52. Ideally, how would you spend your birthday?
